Privacy Policy — Movimentos de Amor

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary

  • Your movement records, the baby's name and the due date stay only on your device.
  • There is no account, sign-up, login or cloud.
  • The app is not a medical device and does not replace your obstetric care team.
  • Ads (Google AdMob) and analytics/crash reports (Firebase) only start after your consent.
  • You can erase everything through Android and turn off analytics in the app Settings.

1. Who we are and contact

The app Movimentos de Amor (Android package com.movimentosdeamor.app) is maintained by Lucian Tavares Martins da Silva, who is responsible for this policy.

Privacy contact: lucian.sistemas.sc@gmail.com.

2. Purpose of the app

Movimentos de Amor lets a pregnant woman record the baby movements she feels, in counting sessions, with history and statistics.

Important: the app is not a medical device, does not diagnose, does not say whether the baby is well and does not replace your obstetric care team. If you notice reduced baby movements, contact your healthcare provider right away.

3. Data processed and where it stays

  • Movement and session records (date and time, duration, count), the baby's name (optional), the due date (optional) and preferences (such as language) stay only on your device, in a local database (SQLite). They are never sent to the developer's servers, nor to advertising or analytics services.
  • There is no account, sign-up, login or cloud.

4. Local storage and deletion

Because data stays only on your device, you control deletion: clear the app data or uninstall it in Android settings. See the step by step in Data deletion (in Portuguese).

5. Backup and restore done by you

  • The app creates a JSON file and opens the Android share sheet. You choose the destination. The app does not send the file anywhere.
  • The file is not encrypted and contains your records. Keep it safe.
  • Restore only reads a file that you choose.

6. Device permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: showing ads and sending crash reports.
  • AD_ID (advertising ID) and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS (Android advertising APIs): showing ads and measuring their performance, according to your consent and ad personalization choices.
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE: used by Google library components (ads and Firebase) to finish short background tasks.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: declared only for Android 12 or earlier, by file components used for backup and restore, always with the file you choose.
  • VIBRATE: haptic feedback (vibration) while using the app.

The app does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

7. Ads with Google AdMob

The free plan shows one banner ad. Before any ad, the app asks for your consent through the Google UMP form, and the ads SDK only starts afterwards.

Google may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) to show ads, according to the choices you make in the form. The app does not send movement records, due dates or notes to AdMob. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy.

How to limit personalized ads

On Android, open Settings > Google > Ads (the path may vary by device). There you can turn off ad personalization and reset or delete your advertising ID.

8. Usage and crash analytics (Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, Google services.

  • They only start after your consent and can be turned off at any time in Settings > “Help improve the app”.
  • Analytics records only three anonymous events, with no parameters: onboarding completed, backup exported and backup imported. It never records health data, dates, counts, names or text.
  • Crashlytics receives crash reports with fixed error categories and technical device data (model and Android version), never text you typed.
  • Google may process an app-installation identifier and technical device information in these services.

9. Sharing with third parties

The only third parties involved are Google services (AdMob and Firebase), as described above. We do not sell personal data and we do not share your movement records with anyone.

10. Legal basis

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018), processing for ads and for analytics/crash reports is based on your consent (art. 7, I). Pregnancy records stay on your device and are not processed by the developer.

For users in the European Union and European Economic Area, consent is collected through the UMP form, in line with the GDPR.

11. Your rights

Article 18 of the LGPD grants rights such as confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion, portability and withdrawal of consent. You can exercise them by email at lucian.sistemas.sc@gmail.com.

Since the developer does not receive your records, most rights are exercised directly in the app and on your device: you view and edit your data in the app, export a backup and erase everything through Android. Consent can be withdrawn in the app Settings.

12. Retention

Local records remain until you delete them, clear the app data or uninstall the app. Analytics and crash data follow the retention periods configured in Firebase.

13. Security

Local data is protected by the Android sandbox. We recommend keeping your screen lock on and taking care of backup files, which are not encrypted.

14. Children and teenagers

The app is intended for adults (pregnant women) and is not directed to people under 18.

15. International transfer

Google services may process data outside Brazil, according to Google's policy.

16. Changes to this policy

The update date, at the top and bottom of this page, changes whenever the text changes.

17. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: lucian.sistemas.sc@gmail.com.
